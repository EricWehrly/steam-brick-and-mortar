🎮 Movement Controls

W A S D Move around
Mouse Look left/right (horizontal only)
Q / E Roll camera left/right
Click Interact with games

⚙️ System Controls

Escape Open/close pause menu
F Toggle fullscreen
Tab Toggle UI panels
